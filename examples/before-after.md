# Before and after

## Example 1 — meta-commentary

Before:

> It's important to note that the backup completed successfully.

After:

> The backup completed successfully.

## Example 2 — information grading

Before:

> Two things are worth you knowing before you make the decision.

After:

> I found two things to tell you before you make the decision.

## Example 3 — legitimate judgment

Original:

> That domain is worth chasing.

Result:

> That domain is worth chasing.

No rewrite. The sentence judges whether an action is worth the effort.

## Example 4 — technical vocabulary

Original:

> The framework uses a rollback mechanism to reduce recovery time.

Result:

> The framework uses a rollback mechanism to reduce recovery time.

No rewrite. "Framework" is doing legitimate technical work here.

## Example 5 — chatbot artifact

Before:

> Here is a concise summary of the changes.

After:

> The changes are below.

Or, when the heading already provides context, remove the sentence entirely.
